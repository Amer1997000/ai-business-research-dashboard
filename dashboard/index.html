Methodology

Controlled crossover simulation

Seventy fictional profiles completed eight tasks through the deployed TEST workflow. Random assignment determined sequence S1–S4. Each participant completed both manual and AI-assisted conditions with matched document sets. Accuracy scores compare eight extracted fields and five discrepancy categories against the researcher answer key.

Limitations

Portfolio demonstration, not causal evidence

All observations, personas, timings and feedback are synthetic. Confidence intervals describe the simulation generator only. They cannot establish real-world productivity gains, human behavior, or AI effectiveness. Simulated dates are scheduling metadata rather than actual participation dates.
